# World Cup 2026 — What My Model Said

A forecast of the 2026 World Cup was frozen before a ball was kicked: every team rated
from ~49,000 international results since 1872, then the whole tournament simulated
10,000 times.

The tournament is over, so it can be marked. **Pick any of the 32 knockout ties** to see
what the model said beforehand, what actually happened, and whether it got it right.

It called the exact final (Spain v Argentina) and the champion. The favourite went
through in 26 of the 32 ties.

Code, data and every figure: https://github.com/Ganapathy-K/fifa-world-cup-2026-forecast
